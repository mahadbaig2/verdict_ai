# 🧪 Verdict AI Testing Guide

Use these example inputs to test the limits of the new **Startup Readiness Report**.

## 🔴 Test Case 1: The "Lazy Kill"
**Scenario:** A saturated consumer app with no differentiation.

- **Idea:** An AI-powered recipe generator app where you take a photo of your fridge ingredients and it tells you what to cook.
- **Target Customer:** Busy moms and college students.
- **Geography:** Global / App Store
- **Pricing:** Freemium with $5/month subscription for infinite recipes.
- **Founder Background:** Marketing student with no technical skills. I have 500 instagram followers.

> **Expected Outcome:** **KILL**.
> *   **Market Reality:** Should flag high saturation and successful free competitors (ChatGPT, Supercook).
> *   **Founder Fit:** Low score (tech product, non-tech founder).
> *   **Killer Risk:** "Zero barrier to entry; feature-not-a-product."

---

## 🟡 Test Case 2: The "Hardware Pivot"
**Scenario:** Good problem, but wrong solution (hardware is hard).

- **Idea:** A smart water bottle that tracks hydration automatically using sensors and syncs to an app. It glows when you haven't drunk enough water.
- **Target Customer:** Health-conscious biohackers and athletes.
- **Geography:** US and Europe
- **Pricing:** $129 per bottle (hardware sales).
- **Founder Background:** 5 years experience in supply chain management.

> **Expected Outcome:** **PIVOT**.
> *   **Verdict:** Pivot (likely to B2B software or simpler product).
> *   **Market:** Validated demand for health tracking, but skepticism on expensive bottles.
> *   **Killer Risk:** Inventory costs and low margins.
> *   **Pivot Rec:** Focus on corporate wellness software or a universal bottle attachment.

---

## 🟢 Test Case 3: The "Boring B2B GO"
**Scenario:** High willingness to pay, boring problem, perfect founder fit.

- **Idea:** Automated SOC2 compliance reporting software specifically for fintech startups. It plugs into AWS and Jira to auto-generate audit evidence.
- **Target Customer:** CTOs of Series A/B fintech companies in New York and London.
- **Geography:** US / UK / EU
- **Pricing:** $15,000 per year enterprise license.
- **Founder Background:** I was the CISO (Chief Information Security Officer) at a unicorn fintech for 4 years and managed SOC2 audits manually.

> **Expected Outcome:** **GO**.
> *   **Founder Fit:** Near 100/100 (Domain expert solving their own past pain).
> *   **Market:** High demand, recession-proof budget (compliance is mandatory).
> *   **Differentiation:** Niche focus (Fintech) vs generic compliance tools (Drata/Vanta).
